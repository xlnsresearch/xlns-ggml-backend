# Dot-product error scaling of the LNS summation modes

Every number comes from the backend's own `mul_mat` kernels (LNSCUDA16 / LNSCUDA32, bit-identical to the CPU backends), driven through the ggml API by `dot-bench`. The kernel gets the original F32 inputs; the reference is the exact long-double sum of the products of the values the kernel actually uses after conversion (`to_f32(from_f32(x))`, read back from the same device), so `acc` measures only what the accumulation adds (LNS multiplication is exact). `total` is against the unconverted inputs and also includes the conversion. For xlns32 the read-back through F32 is itself rounded (~6e-8), which sets a floor on `acc` at small K.

Error of one dot product: e = (computed - exact) / sum|a_i b_i|. Over 4096 dot products per point: `bias` = mean(e), `sd` = std(e), `rms` = sqrt(mean(e^2)). For the positive distribution sum|a_i b_i| is the sum itself, so e is the plain relative error.

Distributions: `signed` a, b ~ N(0,1) (projection matmuls); `positive` |N(0,1)| (sums of squares, softmax denominators); `attn` a ~ N(0,1), b = softmax(2 N(0,1)) over K (attention-weighted sum of V, K = context length).

## Converting F32 to LNS

Every LNS op converts its F32 inputs. 4M values with log-uniform magnitudes over 2^-20 .. 2^20 and random sign, converted once (`to_f32(from_f32(x))`), then twice and three times. Round-to-nearest would give a mean of about 0, equal shares rounded up and down, and no change on a second round trip.

| format | mean rel. error | rms rel. error | mean log2 | rounded down / up | changed by round trip 2 / 3 | drift log2 per round trip |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| xlns16 | -1.43e-03 | 3.54e-03 | -2.07e-03 | 63% / 37% | 49% / 49% | -3.86e-03, -3.84e-03 |
| xlns32 | +2.65e-11 | 5.81e-08 | +3.82e-11 | 24% / 24% | 31% / 20% | +3.23e-11, +2.08e-11 |

### Normalizers on real activations: SmolLM2-135M

Both are long sums of positive terms. In LNS the final multiply (rms_norm) and divide (softmax) are exact, so the outputs give the accumulated sum back exactly:

- `rms_norm` (input `ffn_inp-L`, K = width): relative error of the 1/rms the kernel used. Positive = the sum of squares came out too small, so every normalized output is that much too large. `pred` is (1+b)^(-1/2) - 1, with b the synthetic positive-sum bias at the same K.
- `softmax` (input `kq-L`, causal mask, scale 1/8): 1/sum(outputs) - 1, the relative error of the denominator, bucketed by K = position + 1. Negative = the denominator came out too small. `pred` is b itself.
