Photosynthesis is the process by which green plants use sunlight, water and carbon dioxide to produce glucose and oxygen. It takes place mainly in the leaves, inside small structures called chloroplasts that contain the pigment chlorophyll. The oxygen released during photosynthesis is essential for most living organisms on Earth. The heart is a muscular organ that pumps blood through the body. It has four chambers: two upper chambers called atria and two lower chambers called ventricles. Blood low in oxygen enters the right side of the heart and is pumped to the lungs, while blood rich in oxygen returns to the left side and is pumped out to the rest of the body. Machine learning is a field of computer science in which programs learn patterns from data instead of following rules written by hand. A model is trained on many examples, its parameters are adjusted to reduce the error on those examples, and it is then evaluated on new data that it has not seen before to check how well it generalises.
